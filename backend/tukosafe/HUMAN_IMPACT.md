# TukoSafe — Human Impact & Trust Statement

> **Go Out. Have Fun. Get Home Safe.** The name comes from the Swahili
> *“tuko salama” — “we are safe.”*

## The human problem

Young people in Nairobi go out in groups — clubs, concerts, road trips — and the
moments of real danger are short windows: friends separated in a crowd, someone
leaving with a stranger, a friend falling ill, a journey home that runs too long.
Today, **nobody realises anything is wrong until hours later.**

## What TukoSafe changes

- **Scheduled check-ins + return-home plans** mean a missed “I’m home” becomes a
  calm, verification-first nudge to a trusted friend within minutes — not a
  panicked search the next morning.
- **Discreet Get Me Home** lets someone leave a bad situation without confrontation:
  a quiet “call me” text, a location ping, or a monitored journey home.
- **One-press SOS** alerts emergency contacts immediately, with live location and
  acknowledgement so helpers can coordinate instead of duplicating panic.
- **Safe Venue Network (Plus)** connects the app to trained humans — venue staff
  with a safety protocol and a safe place to wait.

## Trust & privacy by design (why communities can believe us)

1. **Voluntary, revocable, time-boxed** — nobody can be tracked without explicit
   consent; every share expires automatically and can be stopped instantly.
2. **Verify, don’t assume** — alerts ask *“Can you confirm you’re okay?”*, never
   declare someone missing. Status colours always carry text labels.
3. **No surveillance business model** — location histories are short-retention,
   never sold, never used for ads.
4. **Abuse resistance** — mutual acceptance, instant revoke, block-and-report,
   visible “you are sharing with…” indicators.
5. **Honest about limits** — the app says plainly it is **not** an emergency
   service and always offers one-tap 999/112 dialling.
6. **Built for Kenya** — works on low-end Android, low data, mixed connectivity
   (offline queue + SMS fallback), English and Swahili.

## Pilot ethics

Closed Nairobi pilot with informed consent, clear opt-out, no pilot data shared
outside the team, and a defined human support contact during the pilot.
